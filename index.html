Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 28 August 2026

TennisTap is a tennis scorekeeping app for Apple Watch and Wear OS, made by an independent developer (hbjin). This policy explains how TennisTap handles your information. It applies to the Apple Watch app (bundle ID com.hbjin.TennisTap) and the Wear OS app (application ID com.hbjin.tennistap).

1. We do not collect any personal data

TennisTap collects no personal information whatsoever. Specifically:

  • There is no account, sign-up, or login.
  • There is no server behind the app, and the app does not connect to the internet. The Wear OS app does not even declare the INTERNET permission.
  • There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • The app does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, health or fitness data, or any advertising or device identifier.
  • Nothing is transmitted off your watch.

2. Data stored on your device

So that you can resume a match after leaving the app, TennisTap saves the following on the watch itself:

  • The score of the match currently in progress
  • Your match settings (number of sets, games per set)

This is stored in the app's private storage on the device (UserDefaults on watchOS, SharedPreferences on Wear OS). It never leaves the device, it is not sent to any server, and it is removed when you delete the app.

3. In-app purchase

TennisTap offers one optional tip that supports the developer. The purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The developer never receives or sees your payment method, card number, or billing address. Those transactions are governed by:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Permissions

The Wear OS app declares only these permissions:

  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback when a point is scored
  • WAKE_LOCK — keeps the scoreboard visible while a match is in progress
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — required for the optional tip

The Apple Watch app requests no runtime permissions.

5. Sharing

No data is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties, because none is collected.

6. Children

TennisTap does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing that could be collected from a child under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR Article 8).

7. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA give you the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. The match data on your watch is entirely under your control and is removed completely when you uninstall the app. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the "Last updated" date above will be updated.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: hbjin.apps@gmail.com
